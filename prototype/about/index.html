Gary G. Norris, Trial Attorney: Oregon, Washington and Federal Court Since 1982

Mr. Norris has been a licensed trial attorney in Oregon since 1982. He has been licensed in the state of Washington since 2003. He has litigated hundreds of civil cases and personal injury cases to conclusion, from automobile accidents and wrongful death cases to property damage cases. Mr. Norris started and ran the in-house trial department for American States Insurance in its Portland division from 1991 to 1996.

Background

GARY G. NORRIS, born New York, New York, May 23, 1953; admitted to bar, 1982, Oregon; 1984, U.S. District Court, District of Oregon; 2003, Washington. Education: University of Oregon (B.S., Journalism, 1978; J.D., 1982). Lead Counsel, American States Insurance Company, 1991-1996. Member: Yamhill County Bar Association; Oregon State Bar;Washington State Bar Association; Oregon Trial Lawyers Association; American Bar Association.

Appellate Work

In addition to his trial work, Mr. Norris has briefed and argued several cases in Oregon’s appellate courts. Alcazar v American Standard Insurance Company, 256 Or.App. 520, 301 P.3d 978 (2013), Rectenwald v Snider, 134 Or.App. 250, 894 P.2d 1242 (1995), Hinds v Paul’s Autowerkstatt Inc., 107 Or.App. 63, 810 P.2d 874 (1991), Olsten Temporary Services Inc. v Lipton, 104 Or.App.565, 802 P.2d 93 (1990).

Community

He started his McMinnville practice in 1996 emphasizing civil trial work and personal injury. Mr. Norris is a founding member of the McMinnville Education Foundation. He is a former member and past president of McMinnville Sunrise Rotary and served several years on the board of directors of Homeward Bound Humane Society, a no-kill pet shelter.
