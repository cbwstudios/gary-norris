Catastrophic Injury Questions, Answered

The other driver has some insurance, but not enough. My injuries are extensive and permanent. What can I do?

Your automobile policy may contain a provision for “underinsured motorist” coverage. This is similar to uninsured motorist coverage. Underinsured motorist coverage may provide additional insurance benefits beyond the other driver’s insurance limits. Whether this insurance applies is a question our firm can answer for our clients.

How will I pay my medical bills? I don’t have health insurance.

Your automobile insurance policy may provide Personal Injury Protection (PIP) benefits. In most cases the insurer will pay up to $15,000.00 in accident-related medical bills for treatment that takes place within 2 year from the accident. We can assist our clients in processing a PIP claim. We do not charge for this service.

I lost time from work because of my injuries. Can I recover that money?

PIP coverage may reimburse a portion of your lost wages up to two years from the accident. There are several limitations on this coverage. You may also recover from the at-fault party lost wages not paid by PIP.
