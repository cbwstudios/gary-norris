Civil Trial Questions

Do you draft or review contracts?

No. This is a litigation practice. We act when a dispute is already headed for a courtroom or an arbitration, not on transactions.

What does mediation actually involve?

Usually half a day. Each side submits a confidential pre-mediation statement setting out its case and, candidly, its weaknesses and the mediator works between the parties from there.

Can you act as our arbitrator?

Yes. Mr. Norris serves as an arbitrator in court-annexed arbitration and in contractual arbitration of civil disputes.
