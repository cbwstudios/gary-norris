Questions About Oregon Injury Claims

How long do I have to file a claim in Oregon?

Two years from the date of injury for most personal injury claims. There are exceptions, an advance payment by the insurer can extend the deadline under ORS 12.155 but you should never rely on one.

What does personal injury protection cover?

Oregon PIP pays up to $15,000 in medical expenses for two years after the crash, regardless of who was at fault. We process PIP claims for our clients at no charge.

Should I give the other driver’s insurer a recorded statement?

Not before you have spoken to a lawyer. Once we are involved we contact the adjuster in writing, ask that they stop contacting you directly and revoke any medical authorisation you have signed.

What will this cost me?

Nothing for the first consultation. If you hire us we take a percentage of any recovery and if there is no recovery you pay no attorney’s fee.
